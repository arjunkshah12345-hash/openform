## Design Context

Users: People, small teams, and open-source creators building surveys, gathering customer feedback, and collecting leads. They need a welcoming workspace and clear form editor.

Brand: Openform is thoughtful, warm, and independent. Reference: Typeform's conversational form model, with an original identity.

Direction: Light, warm neutral workspace; forest-green accents; fresh lime highlights; expressive serif headlines alongside clean sans-serif controls. Generous spacing, precise borders, playful abstract form-cover artwork. No decorative dashboard charts.

Principles: Make the next action obvious. Treat respondent flows as a conversation. Communicate actual saved/published state. Keep all essential functions accessible on small screens. Respect reduced motion.
