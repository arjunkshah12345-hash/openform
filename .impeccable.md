## Design Context

Openform serves creators and small teams who build surveys, collect customer feedback, and share signup forms. Give creators clear controls and respondents a single question at a time.

Use ink and paper surfaces, acid green for primary actions, violet selection, and compact monospace annotations. Pair strong sans serif titles with readable body type. The marketing page can use expressive dither and ASCII textures. Keep the workspace, editor, and response screens focused on the actual form and its state.

Use reduced motion preferences, visible keyboard focus, and responsive layouts. Make browser demo storage and self-hosting behavior easy to find.
