# Openform launch: useful proof before broad distribution

Source: [Borja’s AI visibility playbook](https://x.com/borjafat/status/2122336732079161572), read November 17, 2026. The applicable ideas are buyer-intent pages, useful demonstrations, a coherent topic cluster, crawlable internal links, clear product facts, and measuring qualified visits. These are a strategy, not a promise of rankings or virality.

## Implemented in this repository

- A real product demo and a motion launch film, with clear browser-demo limits.
- Three substantive crawlable guides: open-source Typeform alternative, self-hosting conversational forms, and a customer feedback use case.
- Canonical metadata, social preview, sitemap, robots.txt, and an llms.txt index.
- A factual feature/limitation sheet so future copy stays accurate.
- Three distinct launch posts, prepared for review rather than scheduled or published.

## Publication sequence after approval

1. Verify the public repository, Pages deployment, social image, demo links, and self-host commands. Never announce a URL that has not been checked.
2. Choose ONE launch draft and attach the film natively. Give the post one job: get interested people to try the builder.
3. Use the second and third drafts as later, independently approved angles only after real feedback; do not post all three back to back.
4. Collect actual questions and failures. Improve the product and guides around those findings.

## Measure without making up traction

Use the same UTM keys across launch links: utm_source=x, utm_medium=social, utm_campaign=openform_launch, utm_content=draft_1 (or 2/3). No analytics tracker is installed. GitHub traffic and Pages availability must be checked after publication; record baselines rather than inventing them. Evaluate real issues, returning contributors, and completed self-host deployments alongside stars.

No unsolicited outreach, paid promotion, automated comments, cross-posting, backlink exchanges, or external account connections have been performed. Search Console access and a real deployed domain are required before submitting sitemaps or claiming indexing.
