OPENFORM / PRACTICAL GUIDES

Build a conversational customer feedback form

A practical customer feedback template with a rating question, a useful follow-up, and a clear welcome message.

By the Openform project · Updated November 17, 2026

Start with a promise

Tell people what the form is for and keep it short. A useful welcome message: “Help us make your next experience better. Three questions, about a minute.” Avoid claiming responses are anonymous unless your deployment and questions support that promise.

Question 1: How was your experience?

Use a rating question from one to five. Name the endpoints in your introduction so a one and a five mean the same thing to different respondents. Make this question required if every submission needs a comparable overall rating.

Question 2: What worked well?

Use a long-text question to collect specifics. Make it optional so a respondent can continue without inventing an answer. One concrete example is more useful than a forced compliment.

Question 3: What could we do better?

Ask for the next improvement rather than a broad list of everything that went wrong. Use a short description such as “A small friction, a missing feature, or one thing you would change.”

Collect contact details only when needed

If you intend to follow up, add an optional email question and explain that purpose. Do not add a required email field to a form you describe as anonymous. Keep your actual retention and privacy practices clear to respondents.

Use the template, then test it

The Openform workspace includes a Customer feedback starter. Customize its questions, choose a theme, and preview it. The browser demo is suitable for exploration; self-host the application when you need responses from other people in one place.
