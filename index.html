Forms worth answering.

Openform is an MIT-licensed, open-source conversational form builder. Create one-question-at-a-time forms with seven question types, six themes, response viewing, and CSV exports.

The interactive GitHub Pages demo saves forms and answers in your browser. Self-host the Node.js and SQLite application to collect responses from other people centrally.
